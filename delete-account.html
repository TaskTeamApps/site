Delete Your Tax Lien Tracker Account

Tax Lien Tracker is developed by TaskTeam Apps LLC.

You can delete your Tax Lien Tracker account and its data at any time, either in the app or by emailing us.

Before you start: cancel your subscription

If you're on Pro, turn off auto-renew first. Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google, and they keep charging after an account is deleted. The app won't let you delete your account while a subscription is still set to renew.

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tax Lien Tracker → Cancel Subscription
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Tax Lien Tracker → Cancel subscription

Deleting your account ends any Pro time you have left.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open Tax Lien Tracker and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Under App Data, tap Delete My Account.
  4. Tap Delete My Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out.

Option 2: Ask us by email

If you can't use the app, email Privacy@TaskTeamApps.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Tax Lien Tracker account." We may ask you to confirm the request. We'll delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, state, and settings such as your theme and dashboard layout
  • Your login, password, two-step verification setup, and sessions
  • Your notification settings and the device tokens used to send you alerts
  • Every entity that only you belong to, along with its liens, properties, loans, sale records, and logo

What's kept

  • Shared entities. If you share an entity with other members, it stays with them, including its liens, properties, and logo. You're removed from it. If you were its only Owner, another member becomes the Owner.
  • Backups. Copies of deleted data are removed from our backups as those backups expire, usually within 90 days.
  • Emails with us. If you've emailed us, we may keep that correspondence as long as needed to handle your request and meet legal obligations.
  • Purchase records. Apple and Google keep their own records of purchases you made through them, under their own privacy policies.

Delete some data but keep your account

To clear your liens, properties, or entities without closing your account, go to Account → Bulk Delete in the app. You can also remove a single item from its own screen.

Questions? Email Privacy@TaskTeamApps.com. See our Privacy Policy for more about how we handle your data.
