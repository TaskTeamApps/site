Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskTeam Apps LLC ("TaskTeam Apps," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our apps (on mobile and the web) and this website (together, the "Services").

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a password. Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form, and we cannot see them. If you turn on two-step verification, we store what's needed to check your codes.

Profile and settings. You can choose to add a phone number and your state to your profile. We also save your app settings, such as your theme and dashboard layout, so they sync across your devices.

Content you create or import. We store the information you enter into the app, such as your liens, properties, loans, sales, entities, entity logos, and related notes and figures, so that the app can work and so your data is available on your devices. If you import records (for example, from a spreadsheet file), we store the data you import. We do not keep the imported file itself after its data has been added to your account. This content may include information about other people, such as property owners or addresses, that you choose to enter.

Collaboration information. If you invite someone to a shared entity, we collect the email address you provide so we can send the invitation and connect their account to that entity.

Notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a device token that lets us send alerts, such as deadline reminders, to your device. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Purchase information. Subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, depending on where you subscribed. We do not receive your payment card details. The store tells us whether you have an active subscription, which plan it is, and related transaction identifiers, so we can unlock the features you paid for.

Technical information. We may collect basic diagnostic data, such as crash reports, device model, app version, and operating system version, to keep our apps stable and fix problems. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may also share crash and usage data with us.

Communications. If you email us, we keep your message and email address so we can respond.

Website. Our website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. Like most websites, the service that hosts it may automatically log basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, for security and to operate the site.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal data unless a feature clearly asks for it and you give permission. Photos are used only when you choose one as an entity logo. Face ID and other device unlock features run entirely on your device, and we never receive your biometric data. We do not use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Services
  • To create and secure your account, including verifying your email, two-step verification, and resetting your password
  • To sync your data across your devices and with collaborators you invite
  • To send deadline reminders and other notifications you have turned on
  • To provide the features of your subscription
  • To respond to your questions and support requests
  • To send important service messages, such as security or account notices
  • To send news about our apps, new features, and offers by email, which you can unsubscribe from at any time
  • To comply with legal obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Collaborators you choose. When you share an entity, the people you invite can see the records in that entity, along with your name or email address as its owner. Likewise, if you join someone else's shared entity, its owner and other collaborators can see your email address and the changes you make there.

Service providers. We share information with service providers that help us run the Services, such as database hosting, authentication, email delivery, and notification services. These providers may use your information only to provide services to us and must protect it. Different apps may use different providers. The main providers for each app are:

  • Tax Lien Tracker: Supabase (database hosting and sign-in), Apple (push notifications and purchases on iPhone and iPad), and Google (push notifications and purchases on Android)

We will update this list when we release new apps or change providers.

Legal and business reasons. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or sale. If a business transfer happens, this policy will continue to apply to your information.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored and processed by us and our service providers in the United States and may be processed in other countries where those providers operate. Wherever it is processed, we protect it as described in this policy.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can export your data, delete individual records or use Bulk Delete without closing your account, and delete your account at any time from within the app or by emailing us. See Delete Your Account for the steps.

When you delete your account, we delete your personal information and the content you created within 30 days. Copies in our backups are removed as those backups expire, usually within 90 days. We may keep limited information longer only where the law requires it. Entities that only you belong to are deleted. Shared entities stay with their other collaborators, and your access and personal information are removed. If you were a shared entity's only owner, another collaborator becomes its owner.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can view, update, and export your information in the app, turn notifications on or off, and delete your account. You can stop receiving promotional emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any of those emails or by contacting us. Even if you unsubscribe, we will still send important service messages about your account, such as security notices and password resets.

Depending on where you live (for example, California or other U.S. states with privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, contact us at the email below. We will verify your request using your account email and respond within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights.

Children

The Services are intended for adults 18 and older. They are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
