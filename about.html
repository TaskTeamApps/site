About us

Task Team Apps is a small, independent studio building apps for iPhone. We believe good software should be simple, reliable, and respectful of the people who use it.

Our apps are designed to do a few things very well, rather than everything at once. We listen closely to feedback and keep improving with every update.

Have an idea, a question, or a problem with one of our apps? We'd love to hear from you on our contact page.
